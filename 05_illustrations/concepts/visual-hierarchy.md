# Visual Hierarchy
## What it is
Visual hierarchy is the strategic organization of elements to guide the viewer's eye naturally through a path of importance : first, second, third and beyond. It answers the question: _What does the viewer notice, understand, and feel at every single glance?_
which prevents cognitive friction and prevents the eye from wandering aimlessly.

1. First Look (The focal point)
The focal point is the hook which commands the highest amount of visual weight, the first thing the viewer sees. 
- Where the eye goes : The region of the highest contrast, largest scale or most vibrant color. Humans look at faces and eyes first in any composition.
